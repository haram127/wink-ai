Frequently Asked Questions

Why Choose Wink?

Fix blurry videos, restore old photos, and upscale to 4K Ultra HD with Wink - the AI video and photo enhancer trusted by 100M+ users. Free on mobile, tablet, desktop, and web.

Is Wink Easy to Use?

Yes! Wink is designed for beginners and pros alike. With its intuitive interface and one-tap AI tools, you can instantly repair, upscale, and beautify photos and videos without any technical skills.

What File Formats Does Wink Support?

Wink supports a wide range of photo and video formats across all platforms.

On mobile and tablet, it handles PNG, JPEG, GIF, WEBP, HEIC, RW2, RAF, BMP, DNG/CR2/ARW, as well as MP4 and MOV videos.

The desktop version supports additional formats including TIFF, RAW, DCR, 3GP, MKV, AVI, and more.

On the web, you can edit JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP images and MP4, MOV, 3GP, M4V, FLV, AVI videos. Wink works seamlessly across devices, allowing you to enhance and restore content anywhere.

What's the Difference Between Wink VIP and VIP+?

VIP:

Available on mobile only (phone and iPad). Grants access to VIP materials, supports batch processing of up to 9 items at a time, and includes a monthly bonus of 100 Credits. Each cloud processing task allows up to 10 minutes of video per segment, with a daily free cloud processing quota of 30 images + 20 minutes of video.

VIP+:

Available on mobile (phone and iPad) and PC. Grants access to VIP & VIP+ exclusive materials, supports batch processing of up to 20 items at a time, and includes a monthly bonus of 300 Credits. Each cloud processing task allows up to 60 minutes of video per segment, with a daily free cloud processing quota of 100 images + 60 minutes of video.

Wink Creative Plan:

The Web version has its own dedicated plan, separate from VIP and VIP+, available in two tiers:

Standard – 1,000 Credits granted each month.

Pro – 4,000 Credits granted each month.

Both tiers include access to all features on the Web version.
